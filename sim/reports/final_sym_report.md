# Symmetric Final-Mimic Map Family: Measurement Report

## Method

The previous 24-map `final_mimic` family was **asymmetric** (left-right diff 36–120 cells). Owner fact: "maps must be left/right or up/down symmetric (possibly both)". Official maps' **walls** are perfectly both-axis symmetric (verified: map1=40=4×9+2×2, map2=24=4×5+2×2, map3=78=4×18+2×3; bomb candidates `cell_code 2` are *not* symmetric and were excluded).

### New family (`sim/maps_final_sym.json`, 48 maps)

Generated by **paired mirror edits** from official map1/map2/map3: each edit flips a cell and its mirror image(s). Symmetry holds by construction. Protected cells (never edited): 4 player-spawn corners + NPC spawn (8,8).

- **Batch A** (24 maps): both-axis symmetric, edit dose ∈ {4, 16, 64, 160} cells. 2 reps per (base, dose).
  - dose=4 → near-original; dose=160 → heavily modified.
- **Batch B** (24 maps): single-axis symmetric (left-right OR up-down), dose ∈ {16, 64}.

**Structural invariants verified on every map:**
1. Required symmetry holds (cell-by-cell).
2. For both-axis maps: `wall_count ≡ 2b + c (mod 4)` (b = axis wall-pairs, c = center). All pass.
3. Protected cells are open.

**Anchor-on-wall bias note:** anchors (6,8) and (11,8) are on the left-right mirror axis (column 8). Under left-right symmetry their mirror is themselves, so a single edit touches 1 (axis) or 2 (vertical mirror) cells, not 4. This makes their flip probability *higher* than a typical off-axis cell. Reported rates below reflect this.

## 1. Anchor-on-wall rate (highest priority)

| Family | n | (6,8) on wall | (11,8) on wall | either |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Official (true sym)** | 3 | 0/3 | 0/3 | **0/3** |
| **A batch (both-sym, real lineage)** | 24 | 9/24 | 7/24 | **14/24 = 58%** |
| B batch (single-axis) | 24 | 4/24 | 4/24 | 7/24 = 29% |
| **Sym family total** | 48 | 13/48 | 11/48 | **21/48 = 44%** |
| Old asymmetric family | 24 | — | — | 14/24 = 58% |

**Verdict:** The 58% anchor-on-wall rate is **NOT an asymmetry artifact**. Under strict both-axis symmetry (A batch, inherited from real maps), it is identical: **14/24 = 58%**. The official 0/3 is likely a small-n coincidence (column 8 in map1/map3 has 0 walls, map2 has walls only at rows 4 and 12, missing rows 6 and 11).

**Implication:** `fixAnchor` (the only consumer of anchor-on-wall on unfamiliar maps) *will* activate on a large fraction of finals-like symmetric maps. The discriminative risk (validation trivially passing because the mechanism never fires) does **not** apply to this family.

## 2. Trigger rate vs 10% cliff

`slowTick` fires when a unit steps on a never-visited cell. Cliff: rate > 10% → P90 jumps 87→130 ns.

| Group | n | above cliff | mean fr |
|---|---|---|---|
| Official map1 | 1 | yes | 10.17% |
| Official map2 | 1 | yes | 10.56% |
| Official map3 | 1 | no | 4.00% |
| **A batch (both-sym)** | 24 | **1/24 = 4%** | — |
| B batch (single-axis) | 24 | 8/24 = 33% | — |
| **Sym family total** | 48 | **9/48 = 19%** | — |

**By wall-count band (sym family):**

| walls | n | above | mean fr |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20–50 | 5 | 1/5 | 8.01% |
| **50–80 (official range)** | **12** | **6/12 = 50%** | **9.69%** |
| 80–110 | 16 | 1/16 | 5.89% |
| 110–170 | 15 | 1/15 | 1.94% |

**Key:** Official map1 (40 walls) and map2 (24 walls) sit *exactly on the cliff* (10.17%, 10.56%). In the official wall-count band [24, 78], 41% (7/17) of symmetric maps are above the cliff. The cliff is a real, present risk on official-wall-density maps — not an artifact of the old asymmetric family.

## 3. Gold collection rate (generated → p1 held)

| Group | mean generated | mean p1 | mean rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Official map1 | 10155 | 2082 | 20.5% |
| Official map2 | 10590 | 2510 | 23.7% |
| Official map3 | 7451 | 980 | 13.1% |
| **A batch: anchors ON wall** | 8698 | 1180 | **13.3%** |
| **A batch: anchors OFF wall** | 8966 | 1797 | **19.8%** |

**Gap: 6.5 percentage points** (13.3% vs 19.8%) when an anchor sits on a wall. Mechanism: `fixAnchor` only fires on wall anchors and relocates them, but the centripetal bias plus mis-anchored units spend rounds not collecting gold. This is a real, measurable cost on symmetric maps.

Worst-case maps in A batch have collection rates of 0–3% (final_A_map3_d160_r0 = 0%, final_A_map1_d4_r0 = 2.8%) — severe under-collection on heavily modified or oddly-anchored maps.

## Old asymmetric family status

The 24 `final_mimic` maps are **not deleted**. Their **effect-size readings** (trigger rate, gold, P90) are invalid for extrapolation to finals (asymmetry is unrealistic). However, their **invariance / identity validation** remains valid: those maps activated `fixAnchor` (14/24), so the "two versions produce byte-identical games" proof on them is genuine. Identity holds regardless of symmetry; asymmetry is a *harsher* input for that test.
